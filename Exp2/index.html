Welcome to Sugarcane Smart Irrigation Portal

The KJS-AGR-01 System provides real-time, data-driven irrigation advice designed specifically for sugarcane farming. By combining IoT soil sensors, localized weather forecasting, and AI analysis, our goal is to optimize water usage, enhance crop yield, and prevent both underwatering and waterlogging.

Featured System Highlight: Stage-Based Water Management

Sugarcane requires varying water levels across its germination, tillering, grand growth, and maturity stages. Our AI engine automatically adjusts daily irrigation recommendations based on crop age and current soil moisture readings.
